## Full description (≤ 4000)
Your surroundings shape your habits. Mise lets each place in your home shape your phone too.

Stick a cheap NFC tag on your desk, by your bed or at the front door. Tap your phone on it and Mise switches into the right mode for that place: social and video apps go quiet for deep work, sleep mode starts at night, your morning checklist appears when you wake, a workout timer starts when you grab your gym bag.

SPACES, NOT SETTINGS
Each place is a space with its own colour and its own recipe. Home shows every space at a glance, with a live countdown for whatever is on.

RECIPES, READY TO USE
Browse recipes for your desk, bedtime, leaving home and workouts, sorted by how many people use them this week. Add one in three steps: adjust the settings if you like, allow what it needs, write the tag. New recipes arrive without updating the app.

A TAP TO START, A TAP TO STOP
Tap the tag again to end a mode. Set an auto-off time, or a strict minimum during which the tag won't end it. In a real emergency you can still end it early by typing a short passage, which is just enough friction to make you think twice.

NO TAG? NO PROBLEM
Start any space from the app. Any NFC sticker works: NTAG213, 215 or 216 are best.

GENTLE, NOT GUILTY
No streaks, no shaming. When you reach for a blocked app, Mise simply tells you how many times you did.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Your spaces, tags and blocked apps stay on your phone. Mise only sends anonymous counts (which recipes are in use) to show how popular each recipe is.

Permissions, and why:
• Usage access: to know when a blocked app opens during a mode you started.
• Display over other apps: to cover a blocked app with your space's screen.
• Do Not Disturb access: for recipes that silence notifications; your own Do Not Disturb is restored afterwards.
• Notifications: timers, checklists and the "mode is on" notice.
• NFC: to read and write your tags.

Mise is free.
